## 本周论文

### 01 · sophistication 究竟是什么？

<Paper
  title="sophistication 究竟是什么？"
  originalTitle="What is this sophistication thing anyway?"
  authors="Eleanor March；James Read"
  journal="Synthese · 208, 156"
  date="2026-09-14"
  url="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-026-05803-7"
  license="CC BY 4.0"
  licenseUrl="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/"
>
  <Fragment slot="original">
    <p>Following Dewar (2019), ‘reduction’ and ‘sophistication’ are supposed to be two different ways of articulating the shared content of symmetry-related models of physical theories. And yet: there does not exist in the literature a maximally clear, maximally general statement as to what the distinction between reduction and sophistication amounts to. In this article, we present a proposal in order to redress this issue: the proposal is to understand reduction and sophistication not as intrinsic properties of physical theories, but rather as four-place relations between two theories, a map between those theories, and a set of symmetries of the former theory. We go on to extol various virtues of this way of thinking about the reduction/sophistication distinction.</p>
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="translation">
    <p>沿用 Dewar（2019）的说法，“约化”（reduction）与 sophistication 被视为两种不同的方法，用以表述物理理论中由对称变换相联系的模型所共有的内容。然而，现有文献尚未以尽可能清晰、尽可能一般的方式说明，reduction 与 sophistication 的区别究竟何在。本文提出一项方案来弥补这一缺陷：将 reduction 与 sophistication 理解为四项之间的关系，即两个理论、这两个理论之间的一个映射，以及前一个理论的一组对称变换，而非物理理论的内在属性。随后，我们阐述以这种方式理解 reduction 与 sophistication 之区别的诸多优点。</p>
  </Fragment>
</Paper>

### 02 · AI 会拯救伦理学，并让伦理学家失去用武之地吗？

<Paper
  title="AI 会拯救伦理学，并让伦理学家失去用武之地吗？"
  originalTitle="Will AI save ethics—and make ethicists obsolete?"
  authors="Max Tretter"
  journal="AI and Ethics · 6, 526"
  date="2026-09-15"
  url="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43681-026-01384-w"
  license="CC BY 4.0"
  licenseUrl="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/"
>
  <Fragment slot="original">
    <p>In 1982, Stephen Toulmin famously argued that medicine “saved the life of ethics.” This article asks whether AI is now doing something similar—and finds that the answer splits. Working from a broadly Toulminian conception of ethics as a discipline that responds to societal demand for normative orientation by providing analyses and reasoned recommendations, the article develops its argument in two stages. The first is diagnostic. AI is undeniably revitalizing ethics: it generates unprecedented demand for normative orientation and brings ethical questions to the center of public and academic debate. Yet AI appears to be on a trajectory toward becoming increasingly capable of supplying the very outputs for which ethicists have traditionally been consulted. If it can provide these outputs faster, more cheaply, and, in some respects, better, demand for ethics and demand for ethicists may increasingly come apart: ethics as a discipline may flourish even as ethicists as a profession face the prospect of becoming obsolete. In light of this diagnosis, the second stage asks how ethicists might respond. Four strategies are reconstructed: (1) defense and counterattack, (2) adaptive specialization, (3) ethics as an activity, and (4) cooperative transformation. Each strategy is critically assessed, culminating in a reflective appraisal of their respective strengths and limitations. The article concludes by arguing that ethicists must fundamentally reconsider the nature of their expertise and the role they can still claim for themselves in an age increasingly shaped by AI.</p>
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="translation">
    <p>1982 年，斯蒂芬·图尔敏（Stephen Toulmin）提出了一个著名论断：医学“拯救了伦理学的生命”。本文追问，AI 如今是否正在做类似的事情，并发现答案需要分别讨论。本文大体沿用图尔敏对伦理学的理解：伦理学通过提供分析和有理由支持的建议，回应社会对规范性指引的需求。在此基础上，文章分两个阶段展开论证。第一阶段作出诊断。不可否认，AI 正在使伦理学重新焕发生机：它带来了前所未有的规范性指引需求，并将伦理问题推向公共讨论和学术讨论的中心。然而，AI 似乎也正变得越来越有能力提供那些人们以往需要向伦理学家求取的成果。如果 AI 能够更快、更便宜地提供这些成果，并且在某些方面做得更好，那么，对伦理学的需求与对伦理学家的需求就可能日益分离：伦理学作为一门学科可能蓬勃发展，而伦理学家作为一种职业却可能面临被淘汰的前景。根据这一诊断，第二阶段考察伦理学家可以如何回应。文章重构了四种策略：（1）防御与反击；（2）适应性的专业分工；（3）将伦理学理解为一种活动；（4）合作性的转变。文章对每种策略加以批判性评估，进而反思它们各自的长处与局限。最后，文章主张，在一个日益受到 AI 塑造的时代，伦理学家必须从根本上重新思考其专业知识的性质，以及他们仍然能够为自己主张的角色。</p>
  </Fragment>
</Paper>

### 03 · 什么是沉积？

<Paper
  title="什么是沉积？"
  originalTitle="What is sedimentation?"
  authors="Saulius Geniusas"
  journal="Continental Philosophy Review · 59, 405–415"
  type="专题导论"
  date="2026-07-20"
  url="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11007-026-09744-3"
  note="收入 2026 年 9 月卷期；首次在线发表于 7 月 20 日。本条为近期专题回顾，不属于本周新发表论文。"
  license="CC BY 4.0"
  licenseUrl="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/"
>
  <Fragment slot="original">
    <p>This introduction to the special issue “What is Sedimentation? Consciousness, History, Language” clarifies the central role of sedimentation in Husserlian and post-Husserlian phenomenology and sets the stage for the essays that follow. Borrowed from geology, sedimentation functions in phenomenology as a concept-metaphor for the temporal layering of experience, habit, and meaning, linking static, genetic, and generative phenomenology. It names both the formation of a sedimented unconscious and the possibility of reactivation, thereby grounding phenomenological accounts of historicity, tradition, and critique. The introduction focuses on Husserl, Merleau-Ponty, Ricœur, Gadamer, and Derrida, while also indicating later reinterpretations in figures such as Stiegler and in political theory. It then presents the individual contributions, which explore sedimentation across embodiment, language, narrative, institutions, surprise, gesture, and psychopathology, showing how it conditions continuity while opening possibilities of transformation.</p>
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="translation">
    <p>这篇为“什么是沉积？意识、历史、语言”专题撰写的导论，阐明沉积（sedimentation）在胡塞尔（Husserl）及后胡塞尔现象学中的核心作用，并为随后各篇文章奠定讨论背景。沉积一词借自地质学，在现象学中作为一种“概念—隐喻”，指经验、习惯与意义在时间中的层层积累，将静态现象学、发生现象学（genetic phenomenology）与生成现象学（generative phenomenology）联系起来。它既指沉积的无意识的形成，也指重新激活的可能性，从而为现象学关于历史性、传统与批判的说明提供基础。本导论着重讨论胡塞尔、梅洛-庞蒂（Merleau-Ponty）、利科（Ricœur）、伽达默尔（Gadamer）和德里达（Derrida），同时也指出斯蒂格勒（Stiegler）等思想家以及政治理论中的后续重新阐释。随后，导论介绍各篇文章；这些文章从具身性、语言、叙事、制度、惊异、姿态和精神病理学等方面探讨沉积，说明它如何在为连续性提供条件的同时，开启转变的可能性。</p>
  </Fragment>
</Paper>

## 学术资讯

### 期刊动态：充分理由原则与伦理学

Joe Stratmann 的 [The Principle of Sufficient Reason in Ethics: Lessons from Wolff](https://academic.oup.com/mind/advance-article/doi/10.1093/mind/fzag043/8801828) 于 **9 月 16 日**在 *Mind* 在线发表。作者考察沃尔夫（Wolff）如何将充分理由原则用于伦理学，区分其中的规范性与非规范性用途，并将其置于休谟式挑战的背景下。

这是一则根据出版社摘要整理的发表消息，供关注近代哲学史与伦理学关系的读者进一步查阅；不是原摘要的替代译文。

### 期刊动态：怎样听取并不确信自己的专家意见？

Snow Zhang 的 [Deference done with certainty](https://academic.oup.com/pq/advance-article/doi/10.1093/pq/pqag058/8793595) 于 **9 月 14 日**在 *The Philosophical Quarterly* 在线发表，属于不确定性哲学专题。文章讨论专家本人也不确定自己的意见是否值得信从时，认识上的信从（epistemic deference）应遵循什么原则，并提出称为 Total Certainty 的方案。

本条同样是根据出版社摘要整理的发表消息。原文涉及不同信从原则之间的形式关系，感兴趣的读者可直接查阅文章。
